The Outdoor Office

Minutes

    How it plays

    Your party and theirs share one office for twelve turns. On your turn each of your people may move and then do one thing: argue with an enemy next to them, take a card from a desk, raid a supply closet, pitch a neutral, use their job's ability, hire help in the Lobby, or rig the projector at the Rock.

    Arguing costs morale. At zero a unit is demoralised: they drop their cards on the floor and sulk in the car park for three turns. Put their whole party in the car park at once and you win on the spot.

    Otherwise, after twelve turns both leaders give a final talk using every card their party still holds. Each slide lands in proportion to how many in your crowd and your party want its taste, times its quality. Their people in the room boo. Best score takes the office.

    Money comes in every turn, and for every break and every recruit. Contractors are cheap, fight for you and leave after four turns. Consultants cost more, give your side a standing rule, and can be poached by the other side for two more than you paid.
